# Arc for Desktop (macOS and Windows)

**Arc is being sunset on December 22, 2026**. After that date, you won't be able to sign in to or use Arc. Please note that we will deactivate all Arc accounts on December 22. Once an account is deactivated, we will delete user personal data associated with the account within 30 days. Not to worry! Many of the Arc features you love live on in Dia, and you can bring your Arc setup along in just a few clicks. To see the full timeline, read [Arc Sunset: Key Dates and What to Expect](https://resources.arc.net/hc/en-us/articles/44412972248471). To make the switch, read [Move from Arc to Dia](https://resources.arc.net/hc/en-us/articles/44413129117591).

New Arc accounts can no longer be created, but if you already have an Arc account, you can still download Arc for macOS and Windows and sign in until Arc is sunset on December 22, 2026.

## Can I still download Arc?

Yes, until December 22, 2026, if you already have an Arc account. To download Arc for macOS, head to [**arc.net**](http://arc.net). To download Arc for Windows, head to [**arc.net/download**](http://arc.net/download) or the Microsoft Store. Please note that these are the only official places to download Arc, and we'll never ask for your device or other passwords while you install it.

## Can I create a new Arc account?

No. As of October 22, 2026, you can no longer create new Arc accounts. If you already have an Arc account, you can keep signing in and using Arc until December 22, 2026.

## I already have Arc. Can I keep using it?

Yes. You can keep using Arc for macOS and Windows until it's sunset on December 22, 2026. Arc will keep getting its usual basic updates and fixes until then, so keep it up to date. To learn how, read [Update Arc for Desktop](https://resources.arc.net/hc/en-us/articles/21489650267031-Update-Arc-for-Desktop).

## What should I use instead of Arc?

Dia is built by the same team that made Arc, and you can bring your Spaces, pinned tabs and folders straight from Arc to Dia in just a few clicks. To get started, read [**Move from Arc to Dia**](https://resources.arc.net/hc/en-us/articles/44413129117591).
