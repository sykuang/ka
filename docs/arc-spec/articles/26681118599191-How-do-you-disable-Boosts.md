# How do you disable Boosts?

*This article applies to **Arc on macOS**.*

You can easily disable individual Boost by going to the Site Control Center at the far right of your URL bar and clicking on the Boost icon for the one you want to disable.

If you want to disable all of your Boosts, however, you can easily do this by:

- Clicking Arc > Settings in the Mac Menu Bar
- Click Advanced
- Toggle off the "Enable Boosts on websites you visit."
Additionally, Boosts with Javascript will no longer be automatically enabled across devices. We recently disabled all Javascript Boosts, allowing you to choose which ones you'd like to reenable.

To enable a Boost on a specific device, just type Command-T and type "View Boosts."
