# Extensions in Arc: How to Import, Add, & Open

*This article applies to **macOS** and **Windows**.*

Extensions can enhance your Arc Browser experience by adding new functionalities and customizations. Whether you're transitioning from another browser or looking to add new features, this guide walks you through importing, adding, using, and managing extensions in Arc on both macOS and Windows.

## Import Extensions

- To Import Extensions from your old browser, go to Arc > Import from Another Browser in the Mac menu bar or Arc Menu > Import from Another Browser on Windows.
- This is eligible for Chrome, Brave, and Edge.

## Add New Extensions

- Hit *Command-T* (macOS) and *Control-T (*Windows) and type* Add Extension* (see image below)
- Go to *Extension > Add Extension* in the Mac Menu Bar or *Arc Menu > Extensions > Add Extension* on Windows

![CleanShot 2023-06-29 at 09.09.02@2x.png](/hc/article_attachments/25701941031447)

## Using Extensions

Use an installed extension by:

- Clicking the Site Controls icon in the URL bar.

- *Right click on any extension to Pin it to the top of the list.*
- Holding Command & tapping E to cycle through your Extensions on macOS
- Typing Command-T on macOS or Control-T on Windows and then the Extension Name to immediately trigger your extension opening
- To make Pinned Extensions visible at all times on macOS, show the Arc Toolbar (View > Show Toolbar)

![CleanShot 2023-06-29 at 09.13.38@2x.png](/hc/article_attachments/25701941038103)

## Pinning Extensions (macOS)

- You can Pin an extension to make it visible in the URL bar at all times. To do this, click the 📌 icon next to any extension. Anytime you hover over the sidebar, Pinned Extensions will be in view and clickable.
- To see even more pinned extensions on macOS, you can also show the Arc Toolbar for more URL space. (View > Show Toolbar)
![CleanShot 2023-11-29 at 12.01.36.gif](/hc/article_attachments/25701921298327)

## Pinning Extensions (Windows)

- You can Pin an extension to make it visible in the Toolbar at all times. To do this, click the Site Control Center icon next to the URL then click the 📌 icon next to any extension.
![clideo_editor_dd5573d5389d4332ba17409054dea1e7.gif](/hc/article_attachments/22079040650391)

## Remove an Extension

To remove an extension:

- Right click on the extension from Site Controls Center located to the right of the URL bar and select "Remove from Arc".
- You can also click "Manage" within the Extensions section of the Site Controls menu to toggle off an extension or access more extension settings.

## Enable Extensions in Incognito

You can enable extensions in Incognito windows by navigating to arc://extensions, clicking 'Details' for the extension and toggling on 'Allow in Incognito'.

## Additional Tips

- Regularly update and manage your extensions to ensure they're functioning optimally and enhancing your browsing experience effectively.
- Explore the variety of extensions available and tailor your Arc Browser to suit your unique needs and preferences..
