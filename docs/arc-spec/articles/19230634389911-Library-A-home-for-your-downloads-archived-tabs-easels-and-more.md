# Library: A home for your downloads, archived tabs, easels and more.

*This article applies to Arc on **macOS***

# What is Library

Library stores all the stuff you've visited, downloaded, created, or saved on Arc. You can open Library from the *bottom-left icon* in your Sidebar, or by hitting *Command-T and typing "Open Library"*.

## **Library has 5 sections:**

1. **Media**: images, videos, screenshots, and captures taken throughout the web.
2. **Downloads**: files you've downloaded, including in-progress downloads.
3. **Easels & Notes**: [Easels](/hc/en-us/articles/19231142050071) & [Notes](/hc/en-us/articles/19233788518039) you've created or have shared access to.
4. **Spaces**: view and move around all your tabs across all [Spaces](/hc/en-us/articles/19228064149143).
5. **Archived Tabs**: search or browse closed and [auto archived](/hc/en-us/articles/19228855311127) tabs across Spaces.

##

# Access Recent Files

Hovering over the Library icon allows you to open and drag recent files. You can edit which items appear on hover by right-clicking the Library icon and selecting / deselecting certain file types.
