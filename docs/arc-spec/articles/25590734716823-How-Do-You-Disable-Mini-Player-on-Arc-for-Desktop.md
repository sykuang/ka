# How Do You Disable Mini Player on Arc for Desktop?

[Disable the Mini Player (Picture-in-Picture)](https://resources.arc.net/hc/en-us/articles/19234766331799-Mini-Player-Watch-or-Listen-as-you-Browse#miniplayerpictureinpicture) by clicking the Site Control Center icon to the right of the URL bar, and then click to uncheck *Picture-in-Picture*.
