# Why Are My Pinned Tabs and Favorites Reverting Back to Their Original URL in Arc for Desktop?

[Pinned Tabs](/hc/en-us/articles/19231060187159) and [Favorites](/hc/en-us/articles/19230755904151) always revert back to the original link you Pinned or marked as a Favorite. Think of them as quick, visual bookmarks.

To change the URL of a Pinned Tab or Favorite:

1. Right-click or two-finger tap the Pinned Tab or Favorite
2. Click or tap "*Edit Pinned Page*"
3. Complete one of the following:
  - Click or tap "*Replace Pinned URL with Current*" to update the Pinned Tab or Favorite to the current URL being viewed
  - Select "*Edit…*" to manually edit the Pinned Tab or Favorite URL
