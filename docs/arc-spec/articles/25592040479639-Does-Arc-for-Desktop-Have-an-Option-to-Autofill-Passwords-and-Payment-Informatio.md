# Does Arc for Desktop Have an Option to Autofill Passwords and Payment Information?

Yes, and you should automatically receive autofill prompts when entering a password or payment information.

Are you experiencing issues with autofill?

Check your autofill settings:

1. Open Settings
  - On **macOS** go to the Mac Menu Bar, click Arc > Settings or use the Command-Comma shortcut
  - On **Windows** go to the Arc menu > Settings or use the Control-Comma shortcut
2. Go to Profiles and select a Profile to review its autofill settings
3. Select Passwords or Credit Cards and verify (or edit) the information
