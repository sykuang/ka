# Arc for macOS - 2021 Release Notes

## 🧑‍🎨 December 16, 2021

- Introducing, Easel: create collections of notes, images, doodles and links or of things you've Captured around the web. Click the square "Capture" icon in your address bar or hit CMD E… "New Easel" to get started.
- Previews for your favorite web apps: Notion, Figma and Linear. Hover over these tabs to view recent pages and take common actions with a single click.
- People Cards now appear in the very bottom right corner of sites like Twitter, LinkedIn and GitHub to learn a bit more about the person you're viewing.

---

## 🐭 December 8, 2021

- Introducing Supercopy. Hit CMD + Shift + C to immediately copy the current URL without tracking parameters or gunk. This is a browser co team favorite, you'll thank us later.
- The Command Bar is now easier to read, scan, and navigate with a fresh coat of paint and UX improvements.
- Clearer page loading! Arc's loading indicator has moved to the address bar, at the top of the sidebar. It more clearly shows loading progress without distraction.
- We've updated Chromium to v96.0.4664.93. If you've been having auth issues, this update will help.

---

## ⛷️ December 1, 2021

- Introducing Auto Archive: let Arc clean up for you! Tabs that haven't been touched after 12 hours (change the timing in preferences!) are now stored in Archive. View and restore archived tabs by clicking the clock icon at the bottom of the sidebar.
- Print crashes are fixed! Thanks for your patience.

---

## 🦃 November 24, 2021

- Introducing the Arc Audio Player! Start playing Spotify or any music streaming platform in your browser. If you switch to another tab while audio is on, we'll add the Mini Audio Player to the bottom of your sidebar. Play, pause, or go to the next song from anywhere in Arc. Happy listening!
- CMD + Shift + R will now hard refresh the webpage.

---

## 🍁 November 17, 2021

- Tab Preloading! Top web apps will stay "alive" in your sidebar so they load ultra fast. We're still experimenting with this and would love to learn how it feels for you!
- Reopening recently closed tabs will also restore their full tab history!
- Continued improvements to command bar and search ranking.

---

## 🐈‍⬛ October 27, 2021

- Notes have a new font! We <3 brand updates.
- The Metamask extension now works to open automatically on webpages! Thanks for your patience.
- Reader Mode is available in beta! Use CMD E … Reader Mode to enter reader mode on webpages that offer it.
**Note:**[The Reader Mode beta has been disabled as of June 6, 2024](https://resources.arc.net/hc/en-us/articles/20498293324823-Arc-for-macOS-2024-Release-Notes#june062024)
- New Arc windows will now keep your sidebar in view by default.

---

## 🕷️ October 21, 2021

- Extensions are now clickable within the Arc window! Hover over your window's top border and click the puzzle icon.
- We've made improvements to search and navigation rankings, but we're not done yet! We'd love your feedback ~ please report moments where ranking in CMD T could be even better.
- This release includes the latest Chromium engine, version 95.0.4638.54, with all the latest security updates

---

## 🍡 October 14, 2021

- The sidebar is now expandable! Hover over the sidebar's edge and drag to expand. Double click the edge to reset back to default width.
- Mini Player can now be turned off more easily. Just click on the three dots in the corner of the Mini Player to disable it for the day or indefinitely for that site. You can turn Mini Player back on by clicking the lock icon in the URL bar of any site to change its settings.

---

## 🎓 October 6, 2021

- Watch videos while you browse! Arc now has a Mini Player. If you’re on a tab that's playing video, we'll keep that video playing in the corner of your window as you switch tabs. If you want to turn off the Mini Player for that tab, just deselect Picture in Picture in site settings (site settings can be accessed via the small lock next to your site’s URL).
- We’ve updated the visuals for Resetting a Pinned Tab. Now you can reset a Pinned Tab by clicking its icon. We've also made it more clear when you’ve navigated away from your originally pinned URL.
- New Notes will now be pinned by default. This way they’re easier to access and harder to close.

---

## 🦉 September 30, 2021

- CMD E prioritizes Focus above Pin (a fix to last week's regression)! Just Hit CMD E & type your space title to focus.
- CMD E has two new actions: Move to Explore and Unpin Tab.
- As always, this download includes improved performance and other bug fixes.

## ⚾ September 15, 2021

- URL formatting in notes (just paste a url onto text and voila!)
- CMD O will move content from Little Arc into the main browser instantly
- A new rev on the Arc logo!
- Fixes to logic for entering / exiting Full Screen
- Sometimes shortcuts between the webpage and the browser conflict. In this update, we will always prioritize shortcuts for your webpage. Hitting the command again will run the shortcut for the browser.
- Support for websites using internal certificates (no longer errors when logging into internal tooling).
- As always, this download includes improved performance and other bug fixes.

## 🐼 September 8, 2021

- Web Notifications are now built! As long as you allow permissions for notifications on a particular site, you'll see them pop up on your desktop while using Arc.
- Finally dock your DevTools! The DevTools window is now attached to the Arc window.
- As always, this download includes improved performance and other bug fixes.

- Split View has a shiny new UX. Hover over the top of each Split View panel to give it a spin.

- We've also added a Little Arc Mac Menu Button, which you can enable in Arc's Preferences.
- New and Improved Plus Button. We've moved the sidebar plus button to the bottom of your sidebar to make it simpler to Create New Space, Add Files, and more.
- See recent history when you long press on your back button!As always, this release contains bug fixes, improved performance and better securityHave a great day!

- As always, this release contains bug fixes, improved performance and better security as we continue our quest for a better browsing experienceHave a great day!

## 🤖 August 11, 2021

- **Tab Syncing Across Devices!** Go to Arc > Preferences (or CMD E... Preferences) and turn on "Keep Sidebar in sync via iCloud". This will sync all of your sidebar tabs (explore & pinned) but wont sync passwords / browsing history. Your sidebar will sync to the state of the last device you used.
- **You can now turn off Tab Previews** that you don't use or need. You can do this through Arc > Preferences (or CMD E... Preferences).
- **Rip a tab off the sidebar to move it to a new window!** Just drag a tab out of the current Arc window and we'll create a new window for you.

---

## 🎾 July 28, 2021

- **Incognito mode!** Hit CMD E … New Incognito Window (or CMD SHIFT N). Incognito windows do not track any browsing history or let you access any of your spaces. Incognito windows are black and have an "Incognito" label in the upper right hand corner.
- **Notes are much faster, 100% native, and have a fresh coat of paint. Oh, and they support markdown!** You can still access notes you've created before, but they wont be editable anymore. If you’d like to keep working on them, copy and paste them over into a new note.
- Use your trackpad in the sidebar to **swipe back to parent spaces** from a nested space.
- Improved logic for the next tab that is selected after you close the current tab.
- CMD E .. Pin will now always default to your current space.
- Improved download speed of app updates.
- Cancelling list rename will go back to original title.
- **CMD SHIFT K** now clears the explore section (previously this was CMD K).
- Upgraded chromium engine.
- **Reset Pinned Tab back to original URL** - hover over a pinned tab and click the Reset icon to go back to the originally pinned URL.
- **Emoji Search**! We now have a search bar for emojis when changing Space or tab icons.
- As always, this download includes improved performance command bar ranking, and other bug fixes

## 👩‍💻 June 23, 2021

- Support for undoing an action via Command+z! Any time you customize a List or Tab, move Tabs in your sidebar or clear your Explore section, simply press Command+z to undo that action.
- Lists can now have background images! We've packaged a few that look great, but you can also add your own. Simply right-click the list, select Theme, and go from there.
- We made it easier to dive into a List! Click anywhere on the list title to enter. If you'd rather expand or collapse the list inline, click the ">" icon.
- More Command Bar speed & UX improvements! As always, this build also includes bug fixes, better performance and security improvements. Have a great day!

## 👕 June 2, 2021

**Files & Folders**

You can now import files and folders from Finder into Arc by dragging them into the sidebar, or clicking the ✚ button in the sidebar header.

Files and Folders can be moved around just like regular tabs! You can expand folders directly in the sidebar to see the files within.

Files can be opened in split view, or you can drag them directly into websites to upload them (e.g upload to Gmail/Slack).

**Other Improvements**

- Find In Page got a fresh coat of paint.
- Improved Command Bar ranking.
We also upgraded our Chromium version, which brings a bunch of performance and security improvements.

---

## **⚾ May 19, 2021**

**Improved Sidebar**

We've got a bunch of improvements on this front. Notably:

- The bottom "unpinned" section is now directly below your Pinned tabs so they are easier to access. We're now calling this unpinned section "Explore".
- We've removed the date grouping from the Explore section.
- You can now collapse your Pinned Tabs.
- To create a List or pinned Note, click the "+" icon next to the "Home" header.
- Selecting a Tab with your mouse is now much faster.
We've also given the context menus for Tabs and Lists a refresh, including the ability to move Tabs to an Explore section on a different list!

**Misc. Improvements**

- You can now easily reset a Pinned Tab back to it's original URL!
- We updated our List colors! Feel free to change them by right-clicking on the List and choosing Color and Icon.
- If you've always allowed Zoom to open, we no longer open a stray tab when joining a meeting.
- It's now easier to navigate directly to a domain.
- If you notice any issues with the Command Bar, you can include debug information to us via the Feedback / Bug Reporter. Just check the box to help us improve Search.
- We added Chromecast support.
As always, this release also includes bug fixes, improved performance and better security.

## 🥑 May 5, 2021

- The **Command Bar** got a fresh coat of paint. It’s also now centered in the middle of the window, making it much easier to use when you have your sidebar hidden.
- Speaking of the Command Bar — we’ve removed those pesky Recent Tabs at the top of the window, and moved them into the Command Bar. The keyboard shortcuts for these (ctrl+tab & ctrl+shift+tab) still work.
- The **Split View** has been rewritten from the ground up, allowing for a more interactive experience when dragging tabs to Split View panes. Pro tip: try dragging a link on a page to a new pane 🔥
- Want to quickly see the sidebar? Simply press option. Want to turn that off? You can in Preferences!
Finally, we’ve fixed up those wonky tab issues when trying to login to a site from a pinned tab.

As always, this release also includes bug fixes, improved performance and better security.
