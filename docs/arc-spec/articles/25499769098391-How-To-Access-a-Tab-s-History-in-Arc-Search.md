# How To Access a Tab’s History in Arc Search

*This article applies to Arc Search for **iOS**.*

To access a tab’s history in Arc Search:

- Tap the Caret icon (^) on the bottom right
- Long press the Back (＜) or Forward (＞) icons to review the tab’s history
