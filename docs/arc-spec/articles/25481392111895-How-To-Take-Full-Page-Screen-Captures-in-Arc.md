# How To Take Full Page Screen Captures in Arc

*This article applies to Arc on **macOS***

To take a full page screen capture in Arc for macOS, press Command-T and type "Capture Full Page".

## Add a custom shortcut to capture a full page

You can also assign a custom shortcut for Capture Full Page by following a few steps:

1. From the Mac Menu bar, click Arc > Settings > Shortcuts.
2. Enter the search keyword "capture" in the shortcut search bar.
3. Click the button icon next to “Capture Full Page.”
4. Press the keys of your preferred shortcut combination.
Tip! Use a modifier key to start your shortcut (e.g. shift, control, option, or command).
