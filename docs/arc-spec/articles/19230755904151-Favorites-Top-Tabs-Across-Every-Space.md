# Favorites: Top Tabs Across Every Space

*This article applies to Arc on **macOS** and **Windows**.*

## What are Favorites?

**Favorites** are easy to access tabs that that live across all your Spaces. When you Favorite a tab, it's icon shows at the very top of the Sidebar, above your Space Titles. You can have up to 12 Favorites in your Sidebar.

**What's the difference between Favorites and Pinned Tabs?**

Favorites are Pinned Tabs that are accessible in every Space. Learn more about Pinned Tabs [here](https://resources.arc.net/hc/en-us/articles/19231060187159-Pinned-Tabs-Tabs-you-want-to-stick-around)!

## How to Favorite a Tab

- Drag a tab above your Space title, to the very top of the sidebar
- Type Command-T (macOS) or Control-T (Windows) then Move To Favorite Tab
- Right Click Tab > Move To > Favorites

## How to Un-Favorite a Tab

- Drag a tab to the Pinned Section or Un-Pinned Sections below the Space Title.
- Type **Command-T** (macOS) or **Control-T** (Windows) then **Move To [*Sidebar Section*]**
- Right Click Tab > Move To > [*Sidebar Section*]

## Common Tabs to Favorite

- Calendar Apps
- Email Apps
- Music Platforms (Spotify, Youtube)
- Social Platforms (Twitter, WhatsApp, Messenger)
- To-do / Notes Apps (Todoist, Notion, Roam)
