# How Do I Export My Data from Arc?

**Arc is being sunset on December 22, 2026**. After that date, you won't be able to sign in to or use Arc. Please note that we will deactivate all Arc accounts on December 22. Once an account is deactivated, we will delete user personal data associated with the account within 30 days. Not to worry! Many of the Arc features you love live on in Dia, and you can bring your Arc setup along in just a few clicks. To see the full timeline, read [Arc Sunset: Key Dates and What to Expect](https://resources.arc.net/hc/en-us/articles/44412972248471). To make the switch, read [Move from Arc to Dia](https://resources.arc.net/hc/en-us/articles/44413129117591).

You can save your Spaces, tabs and Easels from Arc before it's sunset on December 22, 2026. If you're moving to Dia, you don't need to export your Spaces and tabs. Dia brings them over straight from Arc.

## I'm moving to Dia. Do I need to export my tabs?

No. Dia imports your Spaces, Favorites, pinned tabs and folders straight from Arc in just a few clicks, so you don't need to export anything first. To learn how, read [**Move from Arc to Dia**](https://resources.arc.net/hc/en-us/articles/44413129117591).

## How do I export my Spaces and tabs from Arc?

On macOS:

1. Navigate to **Arc > Settings** in the Mac menu bar.
2. Select **Advanced**.
3. Select **Export Data**.
4. Choose where to save the file, then select **Save**.
On Windows:

1. Open Arc's settings: press **Control + Comma (,)**, or open the Arc menu and select **Settings**.
2. In the **Advanced** section, find **Export Spaces and tabs** and select **Export Data**.
3. Choose where to save the file, then select **Save**.
Arc saves a file named **Arc Data** [**Export.md**](http://Export.md). It has a section for each Space, listing your Favorites, pinned tabs, folders and other sidebar tabs as links.

## How do I use my exported tabs in another browser?

Your export is a list of links rather than a bookmarks file, so you'll add the links to your new browser yourself. Open **Arc Data** [**Export.md**](http://Export.md) in any text editor, then open or bookmark the links you'd like to keep.

## Can I copy the links from just one folder?

Yes. Right-click a folder in your sidebar and select **Copy All Links**, then paste the links wherever you'd like. On macOS, you can also hold **Option** while the menu is open and select **Copy All Links as Markdown** to keep the link titles.

## How do I save my Easels?

Easels are stored with your Arc account, so you won't be able to get to them after Arc is sunset, and they don't come over to Dia. You can save all of your Easels at once, or save them one at a time.

To save all of your Easels:

1. Open your Library, then select **Easels**.
2. Select **Export All Easels**.
3. Choose where to save the file, then select **Export**.
Arc saves a ZIP file named **Arc Easels** with the date and time you exported it. Inside, you'll find every Easel you've made as a web page, along with a page that lets you browse them all in any browser. Please note that the export only includes Easels you created, not Easels other people shared with you, and it doesn't include who you shared your Easels with.

To save a single Easel, open it, select the Share icon in the bottom-right corner, then select **Share Via…** to save it as a PNG image.

## Can I save my Boosts?

No. Boosts can't be exported from Arc, and they don't come over to Dia. Please note that you won't be able to get to your Boosts after Arc is sunset.
