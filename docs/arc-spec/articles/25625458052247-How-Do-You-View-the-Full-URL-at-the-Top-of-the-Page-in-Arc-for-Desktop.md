# How Do You View the Full URL at the Top of the Page in Arc for Desktop?

*This article applies to Arc on **macOS**, but not Arc on **Windows**.*

To view the full URL at the top of the page:

1. Enable the Toolbar using the keyboard shortcut Command-Shift-D
2. Right-click the URL in the Toolbar and then click Show Full URL
If you would always like the full URL visible when the Toolbar is enabled:

1. Open Settings from Arc > Settings in the Mac Menu Bar or the Command-Comma shortcut
2. Click on *Advanced*
3. Toggle on *Show full URL when Toolbar is enabled*
**Note:** You can also use [Developer Mode](/hc/en-us/articles/20468488031511) to see the full URL at the top of your Arc window.
