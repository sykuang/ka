# Update Arc for Desktop

**Arc is being sunset on December 22, 2026**. After that date, you won't be able to sign in to or use Arc. Please note that we will deactivate all Arc accounts on December 22. Once an account is deactivated, we will delete user personal data associated with the account within 30 days. Not to worry! Many of the Arc features you love live on in Dia, and you can bring your Arc setup along in just a few clicks. To see the full timeline, read [Arc Sunset: Key Dates and What to Expect](https://resources.arc.net/hc/en-us/articles/44412972248471). To make the switch, read [Move from Arc to Dia](https://resources.arc.net/hc/en-us/articles/44413129117591).

Keeping Arc up to date gives you the latest fixes until Arc is sunset on December 22, 2026. Here's how to update Arc on macOS and Windows.

## **Update Prompt**

**![arc-update.png](/hc/article_attachments/23281349028631)**

- Arc checks for updates regularly. **This is the default setting for updates.**
- When an update becomes available, a banner will appear in your Sidebar like the one shown above. With this method, you are required to click the banner when you are ready for the update to begin.
- Clicking this banner will restart Arc. Arc will close and reopen after completing the update.

## **Manually Searching for Updates**

![](/hc/article_attachments/22076087811095)

If you'd like to update manually, or you are troubleshooting, you can search for available updates on demand:
**macOS:** Mac Menu Bar> Arc > *Check for Updates*
**Windows:** Arc menu > *Check for Updates*

## **Auto Update**

![arc-auto-update-banner (1).png](/hc/article_attachments/23281365550359)

- Arc for macOS is able to update itself upon waking from sleep. Sleep is most commonly triggered by extended periods of idle time or closing the display on a laptop.
- **You must activate this feature to use it.**
  - **macOS:** Arc > Settings > General in the Mac menu bar.
![arc-auto-update-settings.png](/hc/article_attachments/23281349059351)

- Select the checkbox for Updates: "Automatically update my Arc"
- Arc may also prompt you to turn on Auto Update directly after informing you of a new manual
