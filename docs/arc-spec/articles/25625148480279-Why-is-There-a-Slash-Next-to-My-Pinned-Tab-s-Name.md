# Why is There a Slash Next to My Pinned Tab’s Name?

*This article applies to Arc for **macOS** and **Windows***

The slash (/) next to the name of your [Pinned Tab](/hc/en-us/articles/19231060187159) means you’re currently on a different page than the one you originally pinned.

Revert to the original Pinned URL by clicking on the tab favicon.
