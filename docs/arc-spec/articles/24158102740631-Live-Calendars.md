# Live Calendars

*This article applies to Arc for **macOS** and **Windows***

With this feature, if Google Calendar is pinned in your Favorites, you’ll find a countdown timer and a “Join” button in your Sidebar — keeping you on time and making your next call just a click away.

Here’s how to get started:

1. **Pin Google Calendar as a Favorite**
  - Go to your Sidebar.
  - Find Google Calendar and pin it as a favorite.
  - Once pinned, you’ll notice the GCal icon comes to life with a countdown timer for your upcoming events.
2. **Join Meetings with a Click**
  - When it’s time for your next meeting, a “Join” button will appear right next to the countdown timer
  - Click the “Join” button to hop on your call with a single click.
3. **Customize Your Notifications**
  - To change how your Live Calendar notifies you, right-click the calendar icon in the Sidebar
  - Adjust the settings according to your preferences.
