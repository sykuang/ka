# Why Do I Need an Account for Arc?

*This article applies to Arc on **macOS** and **Windows***

Arc for desktop currently requires an account so the team can communicate with Members about product updates, feedback, and questions.

Arc also has collaborative features, like [Easel](/hc/en-us/articles/19231142050071), [encrypted multi-device syncing](/hc/en-us/articles/20272860828823), and [shared objects](/hc/en-us/articles/19228534606743) which require an Arc identity.

Rest assured, [we will never sell user data to monetize our products](https://arc.net/security#faq).

[Check out this video from Josh, our CEO, for more details](https://youtu.be/eVda3zFLlhc?feature=shared&t=334).
