# How To Change Your Arc Account Password

*This article applies to Arc on **macOS** and **Windows**.*

Keeping your account secure is a top priority, and regularly updating your password is a key step in maintaining that security. Whether you're enhancing your password strength or responding to a security update, this article guides you through the simple steps to change your password, ensuring your account remains protected and accessible only to you.

2. Open Arc

3. Fill out your email in the Email field.

4. Click "Forgot your password?" on the Sign In screen (see below).

5. You'll receive an email with instructions on how to Reset your Password.

6. Using your new reset password, go back to the Arc Sign In screen and Sign In.

7. Start browsing!
